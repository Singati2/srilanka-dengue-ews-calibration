# Reviewer response (final v2)

This correction changes the manuscript's description and interpretation of the model contrasts; it does not alter the previously frozen fitted-model predictions.

**§1 — Sri Lanka M1 description.** The frozen Sri Lanka M1 is recent incidence lags (t, t−1, t−2, t−4) plus seasonal harmonics plus RDHS fixed effects (a structured surveillance benchmark); Colombia's M1 is cases-only. The baselines differ by design; the original prose describing SL M1 as cases-only was incomplete. We corrected the ladder to be country-specific; no universal M1 equivalence is asserted.

**§2 — Provenance.** The dated specification defining the structured Sri Lanka M1 preceded the results report; the relevant specification line was never altered; contemporaneous timestamps, checksums, and a hash-pinned report corroborate the analysis sequence. Because the frozen prediction files were stored outside version control, we characterize this as strong but non-cryptographic, and provide a coauthor attestation and a permanent checksummed archive. Prespecification applies to the model ladder and structured M1 only; we do not frame it as strengthening the climate claim, since the prespecified primary (M4−M1) is non-nested. (Forensic detail: Supplementary Reproducibility Note.)

**§3 — Reproduction.** The frozen M1/M4/M5 test predictions reproduce to ≤1.1×10⁻¹⁶ over all 3,926 rows with row identity verified; this verifies computational fidelity, not scientific validity.

**§4 — Principal interpretable exploratory climate decomposition.** During revision we constructed an independently refitted no-climate comparator for the full model in each setting, preserving the same non-climate structure, training and evaluation data, preprocessing framework, and tuning procedure while removing only the climate block. The specification-matched contrast M5 − M5_no-climate isolates climate more cleanly than the non-nested contrast; it was not part of the original design-locked ladder and is therefore reported as a post-hoc, exploratory analysis. Timeline: the Colombia decomposition was in the original submission (supplementary, not the design-lock primary); the Sri Lanka decomposition was constructed this revision.

**§5 — M4 − M1.** Retained, prespecified, design-locked, secondary. Because the models are non-nested and differ in structural components beyond climate, this contrast does not isolate the incremental predictive value of climate information and is not the principal basis for the climate interpretation. It is not "confounded" and is not deleted.

**§6 — M5 − M1 is not specification-matched.** Although M5 and the Sri Lanka M1 share AR + seasonal + RDHS terms, the frozen M1 was fit under a different penalization (near-unpenalized C=1e6) and standardization (AR-only) than M5; M5 − M1 (+0.0081) therefore conflates climate with fitting differences and is not interchangeable with M5 − M5_no-climate (+0.0087). The ≈0.0006 gap reflects the independent refit / tuning / standardization of the matched comparator.

**§7 — Cross-setting.** Both settings show small positive matched point estimates of the same sign (Sri Lanka +0.0087, Colombia +0.0078); these are not of established equal, comparable, or replicated size — under recalibration the Sri Lanka matched increment (+0.0157) is about twice Colombia's. The finding is consistent in sign, not magnitude; cross-setting statements are drawn only from M5 − M5_no-climate and interpreted within each setting's context.

**§8 — Recalibration (estimand resolved).** We distinguish three regimes: raw; cross-fitted ISO-week-parity (leakage-controlled but optimistic and non-deployable, since it uses concurrent test-period weeks); and past-only rolling-52-week (prior-weeks-only, leakage-free, reflecting reporting-delay realism). We do **not** compare an unmatched recalibrated contrast with a matched raw contrast as one estimand. Applying the identical past-only recalibration to the matched comparator, the recalibrated specification-matched increment is ΔNB(recal M5 − recal M5_no-climate) = **+0.0157 (95% CI +0.0066 to +0.0257, excluding zero)**. The Sri Lanka matched climate increment is thus not distinguishable from zero on raw predictions and separates from zero only after past-only recalibration — its separation from zero is recalibration-dependent. The numerically similar frozen M5 − M1 (+0.0154) is reported only as an operational sensitivity.

**§9 — Uncertainty.** Intervals are conditional cluster-bootstrap intervals based on frozen predictions; they omit model-redevelopment, feature-selection, tuning, and training-sample uncertainty. The Sri Lanka bootstrap resamples 26 RDHS clusters (finite-cluster caution). We distinguish conditional, development-inclusive (Colombia, which includes zero), and recalibrated uncertainty, and note the cross-country methodological asymmetry.

**§10 — Threshold.** The matched (post hoc) increment is positive across p*∈{0.10–0.50} (file-backed threshold sweep), supporting sign stability, not significance. The raw confidence interval at the reference threshold includes zero, and we report this without selectively privileging thresholds or recalibration procedures that yield interval exclusion.
