# Financial Disclosure (for the submission system — draft)

[AUTHOR VERIFICATION REQUIRED.] If no specific funding: use PLOS's required statement — "The authors received no specific funding for this work." If funded, provide for each source: funder name, grant number, recipient initials, and a statement of the funder's role (PLOS requires an explicit statement, e.g. "The funders had no role in study design, data collection and analysis, decision to publish, or preparation of the manuscript."). Funding is entered in the submission system, not the manuscript Acknowledgments.
