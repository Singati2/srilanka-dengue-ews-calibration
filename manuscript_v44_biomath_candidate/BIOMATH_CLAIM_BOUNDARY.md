# BIOMATH_CLAIM_BOUNDARY

What the biomath candidate may and may not claim. Derived from the canonical v44 hedges plus the M6/WP4/WP5 firewall. The candidate adds formalism, not claims.

## Allowed current claims
- Climate incremental value is evaluated **relative to a structurally matched, independently refitted surveillance-containing comparator** (matched climate ablation, $\Delta V_C=V(\mathcal I^{SC})-V(\mathcal I^{S})$).
- The matched increments were **small** in both settings and **not shown to be robust** under development-inclusive refitting (Sri Lanka intervals include zero; Colombia boundary-adjacent, selected subset).
- **Calibration and strictly proper scores carry information distinct from discrimination**; a monotone recalibration preserves AUC but can change net benefit at a fixed threshold.
- **Net benefit evaluates threshold-specific decision value**; proper scores evaluate average probability accuracy; **the two answer different questions** and here agree within each setting under development-inclusive refitting.
- **Development-inclusive uncertainty can differ from conditional evaluation uncertainty** (intervals widen when both models are refit per resample).
- Climate **modestly improved average probability accuracy** (proper scores) in both settings on frozen predictions.
- **Forecast-origin admissibility** (WER→ISO date alignment; origin-week climate lags) is a validity condition for interpreting lag structure.
- Exposure was constructed as an **area mean**; alternative weightings are a defined but **pending** sensitivity.
- One scoped novelty sentence (verbatim, with its "to our knowledge" hedge): first application of decision-curve net benefit to population-level dengue elevated-activity forecasting paired with a matched climate-block ablation.

## Not yet allowed (must NOT appear)
- Geomatics **adds no incremental value** beyond surveillance / $\Delta V_G=0$ — **not estimated**; the standalone M6 null does **not** estimate $V(\mathcal I^{SG})-V(\mathcal I^{S})$.
- Any **M6/WP4/WP5 number** (leakage %, geomatics AUC/NB, population-weighted or fractional-exposure results, spatial-CV performance) — firewalled; none are in the canonical manuscript.
- **Population weighting materially changes forecast performance** — WP5 pending; only area means computed.
- Climate is **biologically irrelevant to transmission** — the finding is about incremental *forecast* value, not biology.
- A **formal Sri Lanka–Colombia difference / heterogeneity** — no such test performed.
- **Spatial generalization / transportability proven**, deployment-ready, cost-effective, causal climate effect, or rare-epidemic prediction — all explicitly disclaimed.
- **All satellite data were available in real time** — release latency not modeled; composite-end gating is only a retrospective approximation.
- **M6 is a preregistered model** — chronology does not support it; the matched ablation itself is post-hoc/exploratory.
- Any **mechanistic dengue-transmission mechanism** has been identified.
- **Absolute "first"** claims of any kind beyond the single hedged sentence above.
