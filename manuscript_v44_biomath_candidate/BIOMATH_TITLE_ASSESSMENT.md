# BIOMATH_TITLE_ASSESSMENT

Assessed against the canonical v44 evidence: exploratory, matched-comparator, deflationary; two case studies with **no** formal between-setting comparison; net benefit + proper scores + calibration; **no** geomatics results integrated.

Fidelity criteria: (i) matches the exploratory/matched status; (ii) does not imply a formal country comparison; (iii) does not overclaim novelty; (iv) does not imply mechanism; (v) venue fit.

## Canonical title (baseline)
**"Exploratory matched-comparator evaluation of climate information for short-lead dengue elevated-activity forecasting."** — Accurate and safe; leads with "exploratory" and "matched-comparator." Slightly understates the decision-theoretic framing.

## Candidate title families

### A. PLOS / epidemiology-facing
**"Environmental information beyond recent surveillance for dengue early warning: a matched evaluation of calibration, decision value, and uncertainty."**
- Fidelity: high; "beyond recent surveillance" = the estimand; "matched" honest; no country comparison; no mechanism.
- Risk: "environmental" slightly broader than the climate blocks actually used (temperature/precip/humidity); acceptable.

### B. Decision-theory-facing
**"The incremental decision value of climate information in dengue early warning."**
- Fidelity: names the estimand cleanly; but omits "exploratory/matched" and could read as if a firm positive value were found — mild overclaim risk given the deflationary result. Would need "exploratory" or "limited" to be fully faithful.

### C. Applied-mathematics-facing (used on the candidate .tex)
**"Nested information sets and the incremental decision value of climate information for short-lead dengue elevated-activity forecasting: an exploratory matched-comparator evaluation."**
- Fidelity: highest — pairs the math framing (nested information sets, incremental decision value) with the honest qualifiers (exploratory, matched-comparator, short-lead); no country comparison; no mechanism.
- Risk: long. A shorter variant: *"Nested information sets and the incremental decision value of climate in dengue early warning: an exploratory matched-comparator study."*

### D. Validity-audit-facing
**"Beyond environmental signal: auditing incremental climate information for dengue early warning."**
- Fidelity: captures the deflationary/audit spirit; "auditing" may read as evaluating others' models rather than a primary evaluation; slightly less precise on the estimand.

## Recommendations
1. **Best epidemiology / public-health title (matches current PLOS GPH target):** **A** — "Environmental information beyond recent surveillance for dengue early warning: a matched evaluation of calibration, decision value, and uncertainty." Accessible, estimand-forward, honest, and aligned with the header's PLOS Global Public Health target.
2. **Best applied-mathematics title:** **C** — the candidate title. Foregrounds nested information sets + incremental decision value while keeping "exploratory matched-comparator," suitable for a methodology/decision or math-epi venue.
3. **Most faithful to the actual evidence:** **C** (or A). Both encode the estimand and the exploratory/matched status without implying a country comparison or a firm positive finding. Avoid **B** unless qualified; avoid absolute "first" phrasing in any title.

**Do not** put "Sri Lanka and Colombia" in the title as a paired claim — the paper treats them as two case studies with no formal comparison; naming both is acceptable only as descriptive setting, not as "validated across."
