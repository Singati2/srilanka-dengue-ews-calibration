## Ethics
- **Status:** `[AUTHOR CONFIRMATION REQUIRED]`.
- **Permissible factual description (may be used):** "The analyses used aggregate, publicly accessible surveillance data." This statement of fact is allowed.
- **Not permitted without a real determination:** any claim of formal IRB/ethics exemption or approval. A formal institutional determination must not be invented.
- **Suggested language (only if the authors obtain/confirm a determination):** "[Institution] [IRB/ethics body] determined that this analysis of aggregate, publicly available surveillance data did not constitute human-subjects research / was exempt (reference [number])."
