## Misattributed / correction-needed (summary)

1. **#4≡#5 duplication** — PMID 40901247 *is* Beal et al. 2025 *GeoHealth*; one source, not two.
2. **#2 Lowe dating** — no Lowe *LPH* 2018/2020 dengue-EWS paper; Ecuador = 2017, Brazil = 2021.
3. **#8 AUNBC** — Talluri & Shete's paper is about the **Weighted** AUNBC and argues threshold weighting is **required**; do not cite it as weighting-free.
4. **#10 PLOS NTD guidelines** — mandate STROBE, not TRIPOD+AI/PROBAST by name.

Everything else (#1 Barbados operational EWS, #3 Vietnam superensemble, #6 Mexico AR-vs-climate, #7 conformal recalibration, #9 complementary-research policy) is verified as real and accurately characterized. All ten resolved to primary/official sources; none unverifiable.
